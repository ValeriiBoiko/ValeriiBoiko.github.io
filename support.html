Support Introduction

Welcome to the TrainHub Support page. If you have any issues with the app or need help, you can reach us anytime at support@thapp.pro.

Getting Help

Before reporting bugs, please make sure you're using the latest version of the app. Updates often include important fixes and performance improvements.

If the issue persists, try reinstalling the app — in many cases, this resolves problems caused by cached data.

We usually respond within 24–48 hours. Thank you for your patience and for using TrainHub!

Frequently Asked Questions

To delete your TrainHub account, follow these steps OR email us at support@thapp.pro:

  1. Open the TrainHub app on your device and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen. You can usually find this by tapping on your profile icon in the app.
  3. Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" option in the Settings menu.
  4. Tap on "Delete Account" and confirm your decision when prompted.
  5. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted. This process may take a few moments to complete.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data including workouts, progress tracking, body metrics, and any other information associated with your account. This action cannot be undone.

After you delete your account, all your personal information will be permanently removed from our systems within 14 days, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
