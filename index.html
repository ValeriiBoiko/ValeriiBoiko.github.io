Workouts

A live session that keeps up with you

Start a workout and TrainHub handles the rest: timers, previous results and new records as they happen.

  • Rest timer and Live Activity on the Lock Screen
  • Personal record medals the moment you beat one
  • Muscle balance and 7-day training volume

Programs

Plan weeks ahead, not just today

Build multi-week programs, save templates and see everything on a calendar that swipes from day to day.

  • Workout and program generators to get started fast
  • Import a plan from a file or pasted text
  • Calendar with day, month and list views

Nutrition

Log a meal by saying what you ate

Describe a meal with your voice, a photo or a few words, and the nutrition assistant fills in the numbers.

  • Barcode scanner for packaged food
  • Calories, protein, fat, carbs and water goals
  • A recommended plan based on your body and goal

Progress

Proof that it's working

Keep body measurements, weight and progress photos side by side, and compare any two dates.

  • Weight and calorie charts over time
  • Progress photos with before-and-after compare
  • Exercise history and best results

Questions

Is TrainHub free?

Yes. TrainHub is free to download and use on the App Store and Google Play.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 15.6 or later, Mac with Apple silicon (macOS 12.5 or later), and Android phones with Android 7.0 or later.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. Get TrainHub on Google Play.

Which languages are supported?

English, Polish, Ukrainian and Spanish.

Can my clients use it without the app?

Yes. Coaches can add clients who don't use the app and still plan their workouts and track their progress.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete Account, or email support@thapp.pro. See the Support page for details.
